A few useful details

Questions, answered.

Who is Ripple Mill best suited to work with?

Independent and owner-operated service businesses—such as cleaning, landscaping, pool care and other local trades—that want a stronger online presence and a more reliable customer journey.

Do I need to commit to a monthly package?

No. The initial review is free, and one-time foundation work can be a sensible starting point. Ongoing support is available when there is a clear reason for it.

Are advertising costs included?

No. The management fee and advertising budget are separate. Any campaign, platform fees and budget are discussed and approved before launch.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or a specific number of leads?

No reputable provider can guarantee organic rankings, customer decisions or a fixed volume of leads. We can commit to agreed deliverables, transparent communication and careful execution, then evaluate the results.

How do we get started?

Send a short note about your business, service area and what you would like to improve. We'll use that to determine whether an initial review makes sense.
